# Seed sources

This is the registry of seed sources: the open datasets OTWD imports, or plans to import, as submissions with seed source provenance. A source is added by the steps in [docs/extending.md](extending.md#new-seed-source), and its imports follow the seed source rules in [the moderation policy](policies/moderation.md#seed-sources). The evidence behind each licence reading is in [docs/research/seed-data-australia.md](research/seed-data-australia.md) and [docs/research/road-and-ferry-data.md](research/road-and-ferry-data.md). A licence reading is not legal advice.

## Status

- **Candidate:** the licence reads as compatible and the source is planned, but nothing has been imported.
- **Active:** imported and re-synced on its schedule.
- **Blocked:** not imported until the question in the notes is answered.
- **Retired:** no longer imported. Facts it supplied keep their provenance.

## Re-sync

An active source is re-imported on the schedule in its row, never more often than the publisher updates it and at least once a year. Each re-import proposes only the facts the source supplies, and a run that would change more than 10% of the source's records stops for a person to check it before anything is submitted, because that usually means the publisher changed its format. A source whose licence or terms change is suspended until the change is reviewed.
