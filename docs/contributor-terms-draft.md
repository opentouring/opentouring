# Data contributor terms (draft)

**Draft for legal review, not in force.** This is a plain-English draft of the terms Open Touring intends to ask data contributors to accept. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, it is not legal advice, and nobody is bound by it. Outside data contributions are not being accepted while these terms are a draft.

## Who these terms are between

These terms are between you, the contributor, and the operator of Open Touring. The operator is currently Shane Lord, trading as a sole trader, and is intended to become a not-for-profit host organisation. "The operator" includes any successor that takes over running Open Touring.

## What they cover

They cover anything you submit to OTWD, directly or through a partner app: new sites, facts about sites, corrections, closures, merge requests, photos and descriptions. Together these are "your contributions".

## You keep ownership

You keep any rights you have in your contributions. These terms give the operator permission to use them. They do not transfer ownership.

## The permission you give

You give the operator a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual and irrevocable licence to use, copy, modify, combine with other data, publish and distribute your contributions, and to let others do the same.

The operator may publish your contributions under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0), which is the licence OTWD data is published under.

The operator may change the licence OTWD is published under only to another free and open licence, and only after a vote of the project's members under the rules set by the host organisation. Your contributions can never be relicensed under a closed or non-commercial licence.

This permission passes to any successor operator, including the not-for-profit host, so that the dataset can move to a new owner without asking every contributor again.

## What stays and what you can remove

The permission above covers your contributions as data, and it cannot be withdrawn: CC BY 4.0 itself is irrevocable, and others may already have copied the data under it. What can be removed is anything that identifies you. You can ask the operator to erase your personal information and the links between you and your contributions, as set out in [the privacy policy draft](policies/privacy-draft.md). Your contributions then stay in OTWD with nothing that identifies you. The licence itself allows this: section 3(a)(3) of CC BY 4.0 lets a licensor ask for the attribution information to be removed where reasonably practicable ([the licence text](../LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt)). A photo that shows you, your vehicle or your property can be taken down through [the takedown process](policies/takedown.md), whoever took it.

## Photos

Photos you submit must be your own. You license each photo under CC BY 4.0 as its author, and you also give the operator the permission above. Location data embedded in photos is removed before publication. Avoid photographing people and readable number plates. A photo that shows them may be declined or taken down.

## Your promises

By submitting, you promise that:

- you have the right to submit your contributions and to give the permission above
- your contributions are your own observations or come from a source whose licence allows this use, and you have said which source
- nothing in your contributions is copied from WikiCamps, Google, guidebooks, OpenStreetMap or any other app, website, map or database whose terms do not allow it
- your contributions do not include anyone's personal information without their permission, and descriptions are factual

## Attribution

The operator credits contributors collectively as "Open Touring contributors". Published data records the partner app each fact came through and when it was recorded. It shows your display name only if you are a direct contributor and choose to show it, and it never shows your pseudonymous ID. You agree that collective credit satisfies any attribution you are owed.

## Moderation

Every contribution is moderated before it is published, and the operator may decline, edit or later remove any contribution. Moderation decisions can be appealed. These terms do not oblige the operator to publish anything.

## Partner apps

A partner app that submits contributions on behalf of its users must get each user's consent to these terms before it submits anything for them, and must keep a record of that consent. The partner app sends a stable pseudonymous ID for each user and no names or email addresses.

## No payment

Contributing is free and you will not be paid for your contributions.

## Changes to these terms

The operator may update these terms for future contributions. Changes do not reduce the permission given for contributions already made, and they can never allow relicensing to anything other than a free and open licence.

## Questions for the legal review

- Whether "perpetual and irrevocable" and transfer to successors are enforceable as drafted under Australian law, and whether the split between the irrevocable licence to the data and the erasure of identifying information is drafted clearly enough.
- How the member vote on relicensing should be defined while the host's membership rules are not yet known.
- Whether a partner app's consent flow is enough for the user to be bound, and what record the partner app must keep.
- How moral rights in photos and descriptions should be handled under the Copyright Act 1968.
- Whether the promises about copying should be paired with an indemnity, or whether that would deter contributors.
- Which law governs, and how disputes are resolved.
