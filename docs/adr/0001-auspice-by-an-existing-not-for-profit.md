# Auspice by an existing not-for-profit

Open Touring will be hosted (auspiced) by an existing not-for-profit under a written MOU that holds the project's assets for it with a right to transfer them to a successor entity, sets up a steering group, and covers fees and exit. OSGeo Oceania is approached first, and a Linux Australia proposal is prepared in parallel as the realistic default. Open Touring arranges its own insurance for publishing contributed content rather than expecting it from the host. Data contributors and partner apps need confidence that no single company can take the dataset closed, and FuelOracle, as a commercial customer, has a conflict of interest that rules it out as owner. An existing host gives that confidence, plus accounting and a legal home, without the cost of founding and running a new association.

## Considered options

- **FuelOracle owns it.** Rejected: a competitor of partner apps would control their shared data. FuelOracle is a founding partner and a customer on the same terms as anyone else.
- **Found a new incorporated association now.** Deferred: it adds compliance work before the project has a community. The MOU keeps the option open by allowing transfer to a successor.

## Consequences

Until a host agrees, the project is built under Shane Lord's sole trader ABN, with every account (GitHub organisation, domain, cloud, CDN, AI provider) created fresh under an Open Touring email and kept separate from FuelOracle, so the handover is a set of ownership transfers. Outside data contributions stay closed until the host agreement is signed, because the contributor grant needs a stable recipient.
