# Transferable contributor grant for data

Data contributors keep ownership of what they submit and grant the operator and any successor (including the host not-for-profit) a broad non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to publish it under CC BY 4.0, and to relicense it only to another free and open licence after a vote of members. Contributors warrant that they have the right to submit and that nothing is copied from WikiCamps, Google, guidebooks or any other database. Partner apps must obtain their users' consent to these terms before submitting on their behalf.

A crowd dataset can only change licence, or move to a new owner, if every contribution carries rights that allow it. Without a transferable grant, the project would be stuck with its first operator and its first licence forever.

## Considered options

- **Contributions under CC BY 4.0 only.** Rejected: the dataset could never move to a better open licence, and attribution to every contributor would be owed by every downstream user.
- **Assignment of ownership.** Rejected: contributors keep ownership, and a narrower licence is enough.

## Consequences

The grant runs to "the operator and any successor", so it survives the move from the interim sole trader to the host. Outside contributions open only once the host agreement exists. The plain-English draft is in docs/contributor-terms-draft.md and needs legal review.
