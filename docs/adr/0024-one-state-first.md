# One state first

The concept build seeds and moderates one Australian state before any other, even though the schema is global. A complete, well-moderated state shows hosts, partner apps and agencies what the dataset will be, while a thin national spread shows only gaps. Victoria is first: its four usable state datasets (state forest recreation sites and Vicmap Features of Interest among them) are CC BY 4.0, the same licence as OTWD, and carry the most facility detail. New South Wales and Western Australia follow. The evidence and the per-state ranking are in `docs/research/seed-data-australia.md`.
