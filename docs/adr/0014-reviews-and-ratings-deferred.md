# Reviews and ratings deferred

Version 1 holds structured facts, photos and short original descriptions. Reviews and ratings are left out. They bring defamation risk, fake and paid reviews, and the need for a takedown and appeals process with staff behind it, none of which a volunteer-moderated project can handle safely at launch.

## Consequences

Descriptions must be factual and are moderated like any other submission. Reviews can be added later as a separate content type once takedown and appeals work and Open Touring's own insurance covers contributed content.
